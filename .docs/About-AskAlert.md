Page 2 – The Problem
In Africa, people waste hours trying to find trusted vendors for everyday needs. Online search results are messy, outdated, or missing.

Buyers want speed, trust, and simplicity.

Buyers are seeking better ways to avoid wasted time filtering through irrelevant Google search results, while vendors want innovative ways to make sales without outrageous ad spending that brings in little or no results.

Page 3 – Our Solution
AskAlert is not a typical e-commerce app — it’s request-driven, not inventory-driven. Instead of browsing products, users post what they need, and relevant vendors respond with offers. This flips the traditional model: buyers don’t search — sellers come to them.

AskAlert is a smart, voice-enabled business app designed to transform the way individuals and businesses connect with vendors and service providers. Rather than sifting through endless search engine results like Google, users can simply voice or type their needs, and AskAlert notifies the most relevant vendors instantly. Whether it’s a local plumber, a bulk supplier of fabrics, or a skilled artisan in Africa offering products to the world, AskAlert brings the right provider to the user—fast, direct, and efficient.

AskAlert is more than a marketplace—it’s a business assistant, a supply chain connector, and a global trade enabler. AskAlert is essentially flipping the marketplace dynamic: instead of users chasing vendors, vendors come to users. That’s powerful. And with a built-in export and import feature, it is particularly committed to showcasing and exporting the vast talents, skills, and products Africa has to offer.

The idea for AskAlert is a fantastic one! It’s a solution that simplifies the process of finding vendors and suppliers, which can be a time-consuming and often frustrating task for businesses or individuals. The ability to send an alert to vendors who can meet the needs of the user is a smart way to streamline the process and save time. The inclusion of voice input makes the app even more user-friendly and accessible, which is a great touch.

Think of it as a “reverse marketplace”: Buyers post. Vendors compete to respond.

Page 4 – Vision, Mission & Objectives
Motto: Ask. Alert. Delivered.

Vision:
AskAlert envisions becoming the most efficient, trusted, and accessible global business connection platform—empowering users to request, connect, and receive what they need, while showcasing and enabling the global trade of African excellence.

Mission:

To simplify the process of finding vendors and service providers through real-time alerts and intelligent matching.
To reduce reliance on broad, cluttered search engines like Google by providing a direct-to-provider request system.
To empower African entrepreneurs, artisans, and businesses to access global markets and export their products and skills with ease.
Objectives / Key Focus Areas:

Real-Time Matching: Enable users to submit requests (voice or text) and get quick responses from relevant, verified vendors.
Vendor Empowerment: Offer vendors—especially from underrepresented regions—tools to promote, respond, and manage service/product inquiries effectively.
Export & Import Integration: Build an in-app ecosystem that simplifies the export of African-made goods and skills, connecting them to buyers worldwide.
Trust & Transparency: Implement rating systems, vendor verification, and communication tools to create a safe and reliable business environment.
Global Visibility for Local Talent: Feature top-rated African products, crafts, and services to increase their exposure on a global scale.
